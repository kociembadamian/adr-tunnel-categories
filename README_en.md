# ADR Tunnel Categories

**last updated: July 2026**

Tunnel categories in the European Union and countries party to the ADR Agreement:

Open-source database of **about 500 tunnels** subject to ADR restrictions (categories C, D, E) in the European Union and countries party to the ADR Agreement. This data comes largely from OpenStreetMap services and, to a small extent, from the UNECE (United Nations Economic Commission for Europe) website.
